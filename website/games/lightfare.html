LIGHTFARE

Every look costs. Reach the exit with light to spare.

Walk to the exit with the halo you have. A full look lights the whole board for one second and costs 1, then 2, then 4, then 8.

Light left when you reach the exit is your score.

Arrow keys or WASD move. Space looks. Enter starts, advances, and retries.
