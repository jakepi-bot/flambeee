ECHO ROUNDS

One dark room, four echo cells. Shots are the only light, and every shot gives you away.

Ammo: 16 Time: 60s Cleared: 0/4 Click to enter the dark room.

WASD moves where you face. Arrow keys look. F fires along the crosshair. Space or Enter starts, R restarts. Click the canvas to grab pointer lock and look with the mouse. Controller support is new and unverified on physical hardware. Firing reveals you.
