## Manual test cases (real device / browser)

### Wordfire share (Story 010)
- B1: Solve the daily puzzle, verify Share button appears on win overlay. Tap it. Verify clipboard contains "Wordfire N/6" + streak + 6 rows of 5 tiles, no letters.
- B2: On a phone, verify Web Share sheet opens (navigator.share path).
- B3: Lose the daily puzzle, verify no Share button.
- B4: Win a practice game, verify no Share button.
- B5: With colorblind mode on, solve daily, share, verify shapes (● ◇ ✕) not color squares.
- B6: Deny clipboard permission (or use a browser without clipboard API), verify manual copy box appears with "Copy manually" message.

### Minesweeper per-difficulty stats (Story 011)
- B7: Play Easy, win. Play Medium, win. Play Hard, lose. Verify hub shows plays/wins totals and per-difficulty bests (E/M/H).
- B8: Clear Easy in 30s, then clear Easy in 25s. Verify Easy best updates to 25s, other difficulties unchanged.
- B9: Mid-game on Hard, switch to Easy. Verify no play recorded for the abandoned Hard game.
- B10: With legacy stats in localStorage (flat shape), load the game, verify migration and no crash.

### Website What's New (Story 012)
- B11: Load flambeee.com (or local copy), scroll to What's New. Verify v0.8.0 heading, Releases and Blog links open in new tabs.
- B12: Verify section matches brand (navy bg, red badge, same fonts).

## Edge cases
- Wordfire share with 1 guess (header "1/6").
- Wordfire share with 0 streak (no streak suffix).
- Minesweeper stats with zero plays (all zeros, no bests shown).
- Minesweeper migration when localStorage is corrupt JSON (falls back to empty per-diff shape).
- Website section on mobile width (flex-wrap to single column).
